⛵ Welcome aboard

This is a sailing school built like the Israeli practical exam (המבחן המעשי). Two tracks on the right rail: LEARN — lessons that explain and coach you through each skill — and EXAM · מבחן — the same six exercises as the real test: goal only, no coaching aids, pass or fail.

Finish a lesson to unlock its test. Start with Lesson 1.
